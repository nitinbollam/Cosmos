STEP-BY-STEP CLICK PATHS

Application Step-by-Step UI Testing Walkthroughs

Explicit click-by-click instructions ("what to click, what to fill, and what to verify after") for every financial workflow and core scenario.

1. Accounts Receivable (AR) Invoicing & PDF Download

  1. Log in to Admin Console at /admin/login as admin@pleros.local / admin1234.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Finance to navigate to /admin/finance.
  3. Click the Invoices (AR) tab in the sub-header.
  4. Locate Invoice #INV-10092 (or any unpaid invoice row) and click to open Detail drawer.
  5. Click the Download PDF button in top-right toolbar.
  6. ✔ Verification: Browser opens/downloads a valid PDF document (`invoice-INV-10092.pdf`) generated via `pdfkit`.

2. Recording Customer Payments Against AR Invoices

  1. Open /admin/finance → **Invoices (AR)** tab → Click unpaid invoice row ($1,250.00 balance).
  2. Click Record Payment button. Select Method CHECK, Check # CHK-2026-88, Amount 1250.00.
  3. Click Submit Payment.
  4. ✔ Verification: Invoice status updates to `PAID`, balance shows `$0.00`, credit written to AR (`1200`), debit to Cash (`1000`).

3. Accounts Payable (AP) Vendor Bills & 3-Way Match

  1. Open /admin/purchasing → Select approved PO #PO-4001 → Click Receive Goods → Confirm.
  2. Click Generate Vendor Bill → Enter Invoice # INV-VEND-9981 → Click Perform 3-Way Match.
  3. ✔ Verification: Match status displays `MATCHED` (green status badge) and AP liability posts.

4. Bank Statement Reconciliation

  1. Open /admin/finance → Bank Reconciliation → Select Bank Account 1000 - Chase Operating.
  2. Click Import Statement Lines → Paste raw CSV line → Click Process Statement.
  3. Review auto-matched entries → Click Confirm Match → Click Finalize Reconciliation.
  4. ✔ Verification: Statement ending balance matches GL book balance with $0.00 variance.

5. General Ledger (GL) & Manual Journal Entries

  1. Open /admin/finance → General Ledger → Journal Entries → Click New Journal Entry.
  2. Line 1: Account 6100 - Depreciation Expense, Debit: 500.00. Line 2: Account 1500 - Accumulated Depreciation, Credit: 500.00.
  3. Click Post Journal Entry.
  4. ✔ Verification: Status shows `POSTED`. Trial Balance confirms Total Debits equal Total Credits.

6. B2B Buyer Storefront Invoice Payment

  1. Log in at /login as buyer@acme-retail.com → Open /invoices.
  2. Select invoice #INV-10092 → Click Pay Invoice Balance → Select Credit Card → Enter test card 4242....
  3. Click Submit Payment ($245.00).
  4. ✔ Verification: Invoice status updates to `PAID`, balance shows `$0.00`.

7. POS Register Checkout with Mandatory Age ID Attestation

  1. Open /admin/pos → Open Register #1 ($200 float) → Add SKU SKU-TOB-002 (Cigars 21+).
  2. Mandatory ID Modal: Operator MUST check *"ID Verified"*, enter DOB 1994-05-14, select ID Type Driver License.
  3. Click Approve & Add to Cart → Pay Cash ($100.00) → Click Complete Sale.
  4. ✔ Verification: Change calculates ($15.00), thermal receipt modal opens, stock deducts 1 unit.

8. B2B Storefront Order Placement with NET 30 Terms

  1. Log in at /login as buyer@acme-retail.com → Open /catalog → Add SKU SKU-BEV-001 to cart.
  2. Open /cart → Click Proceed to Checkout → Select Payment Method **NET 30 Terms** → Click Place Order.
  3. ✔ Verification: Redirects to `/orders/:id/confirmation`, order displays on buyer `/orders` and Admin `/admin/orders`.

9. WMS Purchase Order Goods Receiving & Putaway

  1. Open /admin/warehouse → **Receiving Sessions** → Select PO #PO-4001.
  2. Scan SKU, enter received quantity (100), Lot LOT-2026-08A, select Bin ZONE-A-R1-B04.
  3. Click Confirm Putaway.
  4. ✔ Verification: Bin `ZONE-A-R1-B04` stock updates by +100 units.

10. Dispatch Route Delivery & Driver POD Signature

  1. Admin (/admin/dispatch): Create Route, assign driver driver@pleros.local, click **Optimize Route**.
  2. Driver mobile (/m/delivery): Open route #RT-104 → Tap Stop #1 → Tap **Capture POD** → Sign signature & submit.
  3. ✔ Verification: Stop marks `COMPLETED` and order updates to `DELIVERED`.

11. Order RMA Return Merchandise Authorization

  1. Open /admin/orders → Click delivered order #ORD-10092 → Click **Actions** → **Initiate RMA Return**.
  2. Select return qty (2 cases), Reason DAMAGED_IN_TRANSIT, Disposition RESTOCK_TO_MAIN_WAREHOUSE.
  3. Click Issue RMA & Credit Memo.
  4. ✔ Verification: Order status shows `RMA_ISSUED`, stock increases by 2 cases, AR credit memo posts in Finance.
